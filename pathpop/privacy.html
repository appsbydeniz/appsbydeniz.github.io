2. Data the app processes

2.1 Saving your progress (Unity Authentication and Unity Cloud Save)

When you open PATHPOP, the game signs you in anonymously with Unity Authentication. This creates a random player ID; you do not enter any personal details. The game then stores your save file in Unity Cloud Save under that ID. The save file contains:

  • your highest level (record) and your best score,
  • the number of games you have started and when you last played,
  • your player name, if you choose one, and when it was last changed.

Your player name is also stored with Unity's player name service so it can be shown in the game. When connecting, Unity receives technical data that every internet connection needs, such as your IP address, the app version and your device's operating system.

Purpose: keeping your progress safe and bringing it back after a reinstall or on a new device. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the game you chose to use). Provider: Unity Technologies (Unity Technologies SF, San Francisco, USA, and its affiliates), acting as our processor. See Unity's privacy policy.

The game also keeps a copy of the save file on your device, so it works offline.

2.2 Game Center (iPhone and iPad)

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, PATHPOP uses it to link your progress to your Game Center account, so the record comes back after a reinstall or on a new iPhone. For this, Game Center gives the game your Game Center player ID and a signature that proves the sign-in is genuine; both are passed to Unity Authentication. Your Game Center nickname is suggested as your player name until you choose your own.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. You can use PATHPOP without Game Center; your progress is then only linked to the anonymous player ID. Apple processes Game Center data under Apple's privacy policy.

2.3 Data that stays on your device

Your settings for sound, music and vibration and the local copy of your save file are stored only on your device. They are removed when you delete the app.

2.4 Sharing your result

When you tap “Share”, PATHPOP opens your phone's own share menu with a short text about your record and a link to the store. The game itself does not send this text anywhere; you decide where to share it.

5. Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings › Delete my data and confirm. PATHPOP then deletes your Unity player account, your cloud save and the copy on your device, and starts fresh. This needs an internet connection; without one, nothing is deleted and the app tells you so.

You can also e-mail appsbydeniz@gmail.com. Please include your player ID: open Settings in the app and tap “Player ID” at the bottom to copy it. Without it we may ask for your record and when you last played, to make sure we find the right data.

Deleting the app only removes the data on your device. Your cloud save stays available so that you can restore it after reinstalling, until you delete it as described above.
