How to play

Three steps, one rule

Each level shows you a path from the bottom row to the top. Tap it back tile by tile, in the right order. The first wrong tap ends the run.

  1. Watch

    The path lights up one tile at a time, each with its own note. Early levels are quick and short.

  2. Remember

    Then the board goes blank. Press Start fast for a memory bonus, or take a breath first.

  3. Tap it back

    Start at START, end at GOAL. Quick taps earn a speed bonus. A perfect level raises your combo.

What's inside
